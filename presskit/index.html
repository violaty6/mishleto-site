Description

A rogue-lite roulette game. Born unlucky; your life is tied to this game. To survive, buy mementos you want, bend the odds, and return to each spin a little stronger.

TRUST YOUR GUT is a multi-ball roulette rogue-lite. Stack mementos and balls, place your bets, spin the wheel, and survive one more day. Inspired by Clover Pit, Balatro, and Buckshot Roulette, combine Mementos with unique Ball abilities to create powerful synergies and builds. Pay off your debt, make it to the end of the show, and when you've finally earned your freedom, can you stop?

Features

  • Multi-ball roulette with powerful synergies and builds.
  • 100+ Mementos and 50+ Ball variants.
  • Offers that change the rules.
  • Countless ways to manipulate the odds.
  • 4 playable characters with different playstyles.
  • Showdowns that throw your run into chaos.
  • Campaign and Endless modes.
  • Leaderboards to push your score higher.

Description

Park It! is a grid-based puzzle game where players park cars in designated spots across 78 levels. Designed for all skill levels, each level challenges your thinking.

Can you park perfectly without running out of gas?

Features

  • 78 engaging levels: navigate increasingly intricate parking scenarios that require careful planning and accurate movements.
  • Car-inspired mechanics: consider the direction of the vehicle before parking, and optimise your moves with side turns.
  • Full controller support, keyboard-only option, Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud.
